Delete your Catkin account and data

For the Catkin mobile app by Catkin Engineering Sale & Services (Pvt) Ltd.

Customers and riders of the Catkin app can ask us to delete their account and the personal data linked to it. Accounts are created by Catkin, so deletion is handled by our team.

How to request deletion

  1. Email catkin@catkin.com.pk with the subject "Delete my Catkin account", or call us on +92-51-4100081.
  2. Tell us your app username, whether you are a customer or a rider, and the phone number registered on your account.
  3. We confirm the request is really from you, normally by calling that registered phone number.
  4. We delete your account and data within 30 days and let you know when it is done. You are logged out of the app straight away.

You can also ask us to delete only some of your data, such as the photos on a complaint, without closing your account. Use the same steps and tell us what you want removed.

Data we delete

  • Your account: username and password.
  • Contact details: name, phone number and site address.
  • Your saved site location, and for riders, their last reported location.
  • Your solar installation details stored with your account.
  • Complaints and jobs: titles, descriptions, notes and photos.
  • Your device's push notification token.

Data we keep, and for how long

  • Billing and payment records (amount, date, payment status and the complaint they were for) are kept for 6 years, as required by Pakistan's tax law, and then deleted. They are not used for anything else in that time.
