Catkin Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 17, 2026

This policy explains what information the Catkin mobile app (the "App") collects, how we use it, who we share it with, and the choices you have. "Catkin", "we", "us" and "our" mean Catkin Engineering Sale & Services (Pvt) Ltd., the solar installation and service company that operates the App.

1. Who the App is for

The App is used by two groups of people:

  • Customers who have had a solar system installed by Catkin, to report problems (complaints), follow their progress, track the technician on the way, and view bills and payment history.
  • Riders (Catkin field technicians), to receive assigned jobs, update job progress, and share their live location with the customer while a job is active.

There is no public sign-up. Every account is created by Catkin, and the username and password are given to you by Catkin.

2. Information we collect

Customers

  • Account and contact details: your username and password (issued by Catkin), site name, address and contact phone number.
  • Installation details: information about your solar system that Catkin records, such as panel, inverter and battery details, serial numbers, meter number and file references.
  • Precise location: only when you choose to confirm your site's location on the map. The App uses your phone's location to place the pin, and the confirmed position and address are saved to your account. The App does not track customers' location in the background.
  • Complaints: the title, description and photos you add when you report a problem, and the history of each complaint.
  • Billing and payments: amounts charged, what has been paid and when. If online payment is offered, it is handled by our payment provider (see section 4); we do not receive or store your card or bank account details.

Riders

  • Account and contact details: name, phone number, work category, and the username and password issued by Catkin.
  • Precise location, including in the background: while a rider has an active job, the App collects their location, even when the App is closed or not in use, so the customer and Catkin can see where the rider is. A notification is shown on the rider's phone whenever this is happening. Location sharing stops when the rider has no active job or logs out. We keep only the rider's most recent position, not a history of their movements.
  • Job records: job progress updates, notes and the photos the rider takes when completing a job.

Everyone

  • Push notification token: an identifier for your device that lets us send you notifications about your complaints or jobs.
  • Technical data: our servers automatically record basic request information, such as IP address, date and time, and device or app type, to keep the service secure and to diagnose problems.

We do not use your information for advertising, and the App does not contain ads.

3. How we use your information

  • To provide the service: register complaints, assign riders, schedule and complete repairs, and keep a service history.
  • To show customers their assigned rider's name, phone number, live position and expected arrival while a job is active.
  • To send notifications about complaint and job updates.
  • To prepare bills and record payments.
  • To keep the App and accounts secure, prevent misuse, and fix problems.
  • To meet our legal and accounting obligations.

4. Who we share information with

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only as follows:

  • Within a job: a customer can see the name, phone number and live location of the rider assigned to their job. A rider can see the customer's site name, address, contact number, complaint details and photos for the jobs assigned to them.
  • Catkin staff who manage customers, riders, complaints and payments.
  • Service providers that run parts of the App on our behalf, only for that purpose:

  • Legal reasons: when required by law, or to protect the rights, safety and property of our customers, riders or Catkin.

5. App permissions

  • Location: customers, to confirm their site on the map; riders, including "Allow all the time", to share live location during an active job. The App explains this before asking.
  • Camera and photos: to attach photos to a complaint or a completed job.
  • Notifications: to tell you about updates.

You can turn any permission off in your phone's settings. Some features, such as live rider tracking, will not work without it.

6. How long we keep information

  • Account, installation and complaint records are kept while you are a Catkin customer or rider, so we can provide service and warranty support.
  • A rider's location is overwritten by each new position; no location history is kept.
  • Billing and payment records are kept for as long as accounting and legal rules require.
  • Server logs are kept for a limited time for security and troubleshooting.

7. Security

The App talks to our servers over encrypted HTTPS connections, your login is stored in your phone's secure storage, and access to records is limited to the people who need it. No system is completely secure, but we work to protect your information.

8. Your choices and account deletion

  • You can ask us for a copy of your information, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete your account and its data.
  • To delete your account, email us at catkin@catkin.com.pk from your registered details or call us, and tell us your username. We will delete your account and personal information within 30 days, except records we must keep by law (such as payment records).
  • You can stop location sharing or notifications at any time in your phone's settings, or by logging out.

9. Children

The App is meant for Catkin customers and staff and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update it on this page and change the effective date above. For significant changes, we will also let you know in the App.
